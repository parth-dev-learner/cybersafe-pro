Common Cyber Threats

The first step to defense is knowing your enemy.

🎣 Phishing

Deceptive messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

  • Urgent threats or unusual rewards.
  • Suspicious sender addresses.
Verify requests independently.

🦠 Ransomware & Malware

Malicious software that encrypts your files or harms your device, often demanding payment.

  • Unverified downloads.
  • Outdated software systems.
Keep offline backups & update OS.

🗣️ Social Engineering

Attackers manipulating human psychology to bypass technical security controls.

  • Fake IT support calls.
  • Impersonating trusted authorities.
Never share OTPs blindly.

History

Real-World Cyber Incidents

Learning from major historical breaches to understand why security matters.

May 2017

WannaCry Ransomware

A worldwide cyberattack targeting computers running the Microsoft Windows OS. It encrypted data and demanded ransom payments in Bitcoin. It heavily impacted the UK's NHS and FedEx.

The Lesson: Always keep operating systems updated. WannaCry exploited a vulnerability (EternalBlue) that had a patch released months prior.
September 2017

Equifax Data Breach

Hackers stole the personal data—including Social Security numbers, birth dates, and addresses—of 147 million people from the credit reporting agency.

The Lesson: Third-party software vulnerabilities are dangerous. The breach occurred due to an unpatched vulnerability in the Apache Struts web framework.
December 2013

Target Corporation Breach

Attackers compromised 40 million credit and debit card accounts. The hackers gained access to Target's network by using credentials stolen from a third-party HVAC vendor.

The Lesson: Implement strict access controls and Zero-Trust architecture. Vendors should not have access to sensitive network segments.
